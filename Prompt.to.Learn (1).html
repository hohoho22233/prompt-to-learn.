Hello, learner!

What is Prompting?

Prompting is giving clear and specific instructions to get the best results from Gemini.

What is Gemini AI?

Gemini AI is an artificial intelligence assistant that can help you understand lessons, explore ideas, and support your academic learning.

Good vs. Bad Prompts

✔ “I studied photosynthesis on my own. Explain it in simple terms for Grade 11 students with 3 examples, then quiz me to check my understanding.”
✖ “Explain science.” (too vague)
✖ “Do my science assignment for me.” (AI does the work, you learn nothing)

Tips for Effective Prompts

  • Provide enough context.
  • State your desired output.
  • Specify your learning level.
  • Ask follow-up questions.
